ORIGINAL SUPPLEMENTAL NOTICES
=============================

The Windows distribution also includes notices/supplement/index.json and its
648 original files: five supplemental upstream originals for libyaml,
PyGObject, GObject Introspection and JBIG-KIT, plus 643 license/notice texts from
the 359 registry inputs in librsvg's resolved Cargo lock. These include build,
test and proc-macro dependencies; inclusion does not mean every crate is linked.
The JBIG-KIT original is the complete source file containing its license header.
Each file is retained unchanged, including its original line endings.

The index records original source archive names, URLs and hashes, archive member
paths, declared licenses, individual notice hashes, and the verified source
delivery manifest. It supplements the existing notices/native and
notices/pyenchant collections and the license texts above; it does not replace
them or relicense their contents. Paths in this document are relative to the
PyInstaller _internal data directory.

https://dayquay.trieflow.com/source is the canonical landing page for the
application source, exact dependency source inputs, build recipes and original
notices. Retained originals in this package are a notice supplement, not a
substitute for delivering the corresponding source available through that page.
